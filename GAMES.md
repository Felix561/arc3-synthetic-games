# The collection

Each game has seven levels. These short introductions describe a theme, not a solution.

| ID | Title | Controls | Introduction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SG01 | Keys and Currents | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Explore a route where collected keys open gates and currents carry you beyond a single step. |
| SG02 | Color Conduits | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Carry color through an unfinished network and discover how neighboring channels bring its receivers to life. |
| SG03 | Contact Chain | Space, Click, Undo | A single impact follows the facing edges of connected tiles; change the chain before setting it in motion. |
| SG04 | Light Relay | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Redirect colored beams through mirrors, splitters and relays toward their matching receivers. |
| SG05 | Coupled Garden | Click, Undo | A pool can feed several neighboring sprouts, so one watering may change more than one plant. |
| SG06 | Suspended Weights | Click, Undo | Move shared weights between connected supports to release the routes held by their balance beams. |
| SG07 | Layer Press | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Build a picture through shaped impressions, hidden ink layers and a stencil that controls where marks can land. |
| SG08 | Toll Vaults | Arrows, Click, Undo | Every doorway exchanges part of your purse, making the route and the resources inseparable. |
| SG09 | Spectral Causeway | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Steer a two-part hull over changing causeways while distant crystals respond only along a clear sightline. |
| SG10 | Charge Rail | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | A locomotive shares its route with an electrical network whose switches and stored charge outlast a pulse. |
| SG11 | Shared Loom | Space, Click, Undo | Weave several rows against a single over-and-under rhythm, with limited knots that alter what follows. |
| SG12 | Thermal Passage | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Push temperature-bearing blocks through a room where heat and cold reshape the ground beneath them. |
| SG13 | Turnover Trays | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Transfer two-sided packets between shallow trays, watching both face and order reverse together. |
| SG14 | Carrying Window | Arrows, Undo | A moving window carries what it encloses and leaves behind what its surroundings will not let through. |
| SG15 | Ribbon Fusion | Arrows, Space, Click, Undo | Merge neighboring ribbons while their widths persist and their colors advance through a cycle. |
| SG16 | Cut and Fall | Click, Undo | Cut a connected shape at its cracks and let the freed fragments settle onto the supports below. |
| SG17 | Direction Stations | Arrows, Space, Undo | Set directions at programming pads, then advance all pieces together through a shared space. |
| SG18 | Pivot Reflections | Click, Undo | Use one piece as a center of reflection while occupied destinations and walls selectively hold the others in place. |
| SG19 | Capacity Ring | Click, Undo | Redistribute countable contents around a ring whose full bowls divert the flow. |
| SG20 | Borrowed Hue | Click, Undo | Change one tile and watch every tile already wearing its next color borrow the color it leaves behind. |
| SG21 | Hidden Cups | Space, Click, Undo | Rearrange neighboring cups while keeping track of the colors carried beneath their covers. |
| SG22 | Slack Tether | Arrows, Click, Undo | Move either end of a tether that allows independent travel until its slack runs out. |
| SG23 | Clutch Gears | Space, Click, Undo | Turn a gear train whose contacts reverse motion and whose clutches can separate the chain. |
| SG24 | Portable Bridge | Arrows, Space, Undo | Carry a single rigid bridge across a canyon and turn it into the support needed for the next crossing. |
| SG25 | Gravity Vessel | Arrows, Space, Undo | Change the direction of gravity inside a vessel, using one gate to route rigid grains into its wells. |
| SG26 | Equalizing Basins | Click, Undo | Open local connections between water basins and arrange their levels without changing the total volume. |
| SG27 | Opposed Press | Arrows, Space, Undo | Position a two-sided press so one expansion pushes contacted cargo in opposite directions. |
| SG28 | Collision Lane | Space, Click, Undo | Time a pair of runners and spring bumpers along a lane where impacts exchange their motion. |
| SG29 | Two-Ended Convoy | Arrows, Space, Undo | Guide an ordered chain through branching roads by handing leadership from one end to the other. |
| SG30 | Torque Mobile | Click, Undo | Move hanging weights along suspended beams and balance the effects of mass and distance from each pivot. |
